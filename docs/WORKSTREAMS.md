## Immediate sequence

1. Resolve founder/legal/payment gates that block customer-facing launch.
2. Obtain and pay only for approved sample baskets.
3. Physically inspect and test samples.
4. Lock launch SKUs and supplier agreements.
5. Complete branding execution and compliance labels.
6. Finish Wix visual/store policies and payment connection.
7. Run blind fulfilment and fake-order rehearsals.
8. Publish the final pre-launch sequence.
9. Soft launch.
10. Scale only after real conversion, contribution and damage/return data exist.

## What is deliberately not here

The public repository does not contain:
- supplier-by-supplier negotiated rates
- supplier private contact details
- customer/order/payment records
- private legal/accounting documents
- private catalogues and agreements

Those belong in the authenticated private layer.
